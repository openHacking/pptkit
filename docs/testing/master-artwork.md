## Adversarial review and open gates

Five likely regressions drive the checks: flattening shared objects; losing resources from hidden/unused owners; partial ID/allocator commits on failed replacement; conflating layout/page visibility with paint or geometry; and omitting chart/workbook or cross-owner relationships. Native checks additionally prevent accepting a shared-resource rename as loss or ignoring an unrelated semantic change. Source compatibility, failure behavior, dependency direction and unnecessary abstraction were reviewed.

Master table/chart native appearance, broader native connector behavior and actual shared-artwork editing still require their own native controls. Full template/reset/rebinding, per-object overrides, source artwork/visibility import/writes and notes/fields remain open. Fresh ordinary authoring and source-only no-op do not replace those gates.
