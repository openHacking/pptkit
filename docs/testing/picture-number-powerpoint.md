# Picture replacement and starting slide number: PowerPoint evidence

This is evidence for one generated sample, not a general Office compatibility claim. The sample has two pictures sharing one source PNG, both cropped 12.5% on the left. The combined import-session save replaces only `target` with the transparent PNG fixture and changes the starting slide number to 17. The second picture, `sibling`, remains on the original media. A second slide says “Control page”.

Generate the source and candidate with `node scripts/prepare-picture-number-native.mjs .tmp/picture-number-powerpoint`. Independently verify the generated candidate with `python3 scripts/research/verify-picture-number.py .tmp/picture-number-powerpoint/source.pptx .tmp/picture-number-powerpoint/candidate.pptx tests/fixtures/transparent-replacement.png`. The latter checks the expected part-level changes, relationships, media bytes, names and crop; it is specific to this fixture.

On macOS, Microsoft PowerPoint 16.104 opened the candidate without a repair prompt. The first thumbnail was numbered 17; the left transparent red replacement and the right four-color original were visible. Three rounds cut the first slide, pasted it after the remaining slide, saved, closed, and reopened the file. The picture page was respectively second, first and second after reopening; the displayed range remained 17–18. Screenshots and round files were recorded under `.tmp/picture-number-powerpoint/` (`open.png`, `reopen1.png` through `reopen3.png`, `round1.pptx` through `round3.pptx`). These local artifacts are not a substitute for a reproducible test fixture and may be removed with `.tmp`.

Independent ZIP/XML inspection of each saved round found `firstSlideNum=17`, exactly the two named pictures on the expected page, and left crop `12500` on each. Resolving each picture relationship and hashing its media confirmed that the target retained the replacement bytes and the sibling retained the original shared PNG bytes in all three rounds. PowerPoint renamed the media parts; the check followed relationships instead of assuming paths. This test does not establish native picture-editing behavior or coverage of other image formats, crops, Office versions, or combinations.

The saved rounds can be independently checked against the already-verified candidate using `python3 scripts/research/verify-picture-number-native.py .tmp/picture-number-powerpoint/candidate.pptx .tmp/picture-number-powerpoint/round1.pptx 1`, with position `0` for round 2 and `1` for round 3. The checker resolves presentation slide order and picture relationships and compares media hashes, all four crop values and geometry. Run `pnpm test:picture-number-evidence` to exercise both evidence checkers against intentional corruptions; the native checker expects the candidate to have passed the source/candidate check first.
