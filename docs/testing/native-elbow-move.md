# PowerPoint attached elbow reroute

PowerPoint 16.104 (16.104.25121423) on macOS 26.5 (25F71) opened one PPTKit-generated slide with two rectangles and a single-elbow native connector. No repair dialog was observed. The right target was dragged upward and right. The visible connector changed from one bend to two bends and met the moved target horizontally; after save, close and one reopen the rerouted connector remained visible. This is one interactive sample, not a general routing or fidelity guarantee.

Independent XML inspection finds `bentConnector2` before and `bentConnector3` with `adj1=50000` afterward; both ends retain references to the same respective shapes and cardinal site indices 3 and 1. The left frame is unchanged, the right frame keeps its size and moves from `(7620000, 4318000)` to `(8017565, 3155122)` EMU. The saved connector endpoints match the two frame sites. The visible reroute is therefore not preservation of the authored bend.
