# Date removal: LibreOfficeDev observation

A PPTKit-authored control contains title `Date removal control`, subject `Sentinel`, revision 7 and two typed dates. Its created date was changed lexically from `2020-02-29T02:00:00.120Z` to the equivalent `2020-02-29T10:00:00.1200+08:00`; the modified date was `2021-01-02T03:04:05.123Z`. PPTKit's `metadata-date-remove-v14` removed both dates in an edited copy. Independent ZIP/XML checks confirm that only the core metadata part changed and its non-target bytes stayed identical.

LibreOfficeDev 26.8.0.0.alpha0 headlessly resaved the control and edited copy three times. The two dates remained absent in the edited branch. In the control, LibreOffice converted the offset to UTC and discarded both nonzero fractional seconds on its first save; the second and third saves retained those second-level values. Both branches retained the title, subject and revision. This is one synthetic Transitional sample and does not establish general cross-application fidelity.

Artifacts are under `.tmp/metadata-date-remove-libreoffice`; rerun `python3 scripts/research/metadata-date-remove-libreoffice.py` in the recorded environment.
