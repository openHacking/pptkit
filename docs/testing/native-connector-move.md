# PowerPoint straight-connector target movement

On macOS 26.5 (25F71), PowerPoint 16.104 (16.104.25121423) opened one PPTKit-generated slide with two rectangular shapes and a native straight connector attached from the left shape's right site to the right shape's left site. No repair dialog was observed. The right shape was dragged down and slightly right in the slide editor, then the file was saved, closed and reopened. The connector visibly followed the target during the drag and still met it on reopen. This is one interactive observation, not a visual-fidelity measurement or proof for elbows, rotated targets, other applications or other versions.

The retained [before](../../tests/fixtures/native-connector-move-before.pptx) and [after](../../tests/fixtures/native-connector-move-after.pptx) packages have SHA-256 `236e395583f6dad2a3665c1133d92de3392c187e5c61858aedaee79a62f2e1ba` and `81901be601596dd5f4ee47e2282e1191d6df0edbee34eb7afbe8cf0f4c73fbf2`. Independent ZIP/XML inspection confirms both `p:cxnSp` attachments retain the target drawing IDs and site indices 3 and 1. The left frame and right frame size remain fixed. The right frame origin changes from `(7620000, 2667000)` to `(7818782, 3998843)` EMU; the connector's endpoints correspond to the right and left cardinal sites of the two frames before and after the edit. The verifier checks these relationships rather than relying on drawing IDs having globally meaningful values.

Run `python3 scripts/research/verify-native-connector-move.py` for the retained hashes and independent XML checks, and `python3 scripts/research/test_native_connector_move.py` for controls that corrupt bindings, sites, geometry and inventory. [The preparation script](../../scripts/prepare-native-connector-interaction.mjs) creates a fresh source and manifest in a new local directory; running it does not reproduce the manual PowerPoint operation. The local captures `.tmp/native-connector-interaction/run-pF7EAN/moved.png` and `reopen.png` have respective SHA-256 `b6ca2ceb07554e0eb7af4d7e86685f5972b693be4d555872bec1b4a4c24f8126` and `26920e79447481f5c119266f95c6f5ec639d301dad55a81b00c484e1990bea20`. Captures are observational evidence and their hashes do not independently authenticate their provenance; the local temporary directory is not a permanent fixture.

This experiment does not authorize PPTKit source-preserving connector edits, establish native rotation behavior, or complete AUT-02/REL-02. It also does not cover more than one native save/reopen round.
